## W23 — Major Transition, Deprecation and Retirement

**Purpose:** Avoid stranding users, data or dependent systems.

**Use when:** A major compatibility change, feature retirement or end of life. **Skip when:** A backward-compatible internal change without lifecycle consequences.

| Prompt | Your response |
|---|---|
| **Change and affected consumers**<br>Versions/users, APIs, formats/plugins, channels and scheduled jobs; last maintained version, minimum compatible version, oldest upgrade source and last platform/runtime. |  |
| **Compatibility and transition**<br>[W35](W35_Installed-Version_and_Upgrade_Plan.md#w35) old/new matrix; distinct maintenance/runtime/upgrade promises; coexistence, direct/intermediate routes, pilot, downgrade/refusal and supported fallback. |  |
| **Data export and retention**<br>Usable export, migration/restore, deletion/retention obligations and evidence. |  |
| **Notice and support commitments**<br>Audience, content, authorized dates, limitations and responsible contact. |  |
| **Resources and access**<br>Accounts, credentials, domains, hosting, paid services and dependencies. |  |
| **Validation and shutdown sequence**<br>Verify transition before revocation/deletion; archive appropriate final artifacts/docs. |  |
| **Approval and final state**<br>Owner, remaining risks, unsupported status, completion evidence and unresolved obligations. |  |

**Close the worksheet when:** No shutdown step removes access or data before its dependencies are understood.

**Use with:** Chapter 17; [W19](W19_Migration_Backup_and_Recovery_Plan.md#w19); [P29](../prompts/P29.md#p29).
