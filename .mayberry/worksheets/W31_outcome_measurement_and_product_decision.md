## W31 — Outcome Measurement and Product Decision

**Purpose:** Connect the original problem to post-release evidence of usefulness.

**Use when:** A product decision depends on task success, effort, usage, friction or ongoing feature value. **Skip when:** A disposable/private task can be evaluated directly without a separate record.

| Prompt | Your response |
|---|---|
| **Decision and outcome**<br>Requirement/user/problem; retain/improve/simplify/remove/investigate decision that evidence will inform. |  |
| **Metric contract**<br>Unit/population/window; numerator/denominator; exclusions; baseline; owner; do not substitute clicks for success. |  |
| **Evidence method**<br>Observation/interview/manual comparison/events; primary outcome and harm guardrail; smallest adequate method. |  |
| **Event and data-quality rules**<br>Names/triggers/schema versions; duplicate/missing/test traffic; fixture/query checks; known blind spots. |  |
| **Privacy and exposure**<br>Minimum fields, destinations, access, retention/deletion, applicable permissions/disclosures. |  |
| **Comparison and uncertainty**<br>Comparable cases, confounders, sample limits, stopping rule; do not infer causation from before/after alone. |  |
| **Observed results and interpretation**<br>Counts/calculations and evidence links; qualitative observations; facts vs explanations; unmeasured outcomes. |  |
| **Decision, owner and next check**<br>Retain/change/remove or insufficient evidence; rationale, roadmap link and revisit trigger. |  |

**Close the worksheet when:** The measurement plan is approved before collection. Close the outcome review only when observed evidence and limits support a recorded product decision—or an explicit insufficient-evidence decision with the next observation.
