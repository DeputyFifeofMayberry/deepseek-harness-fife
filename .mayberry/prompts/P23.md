## P23 — Post-release incident investigation

**Use when:** Users are affected or a release behaved unexpectedly.

**Inputs:** Impact, timeline, versions, sanitized signals and authorized access.

```text
Investigate [incident] using available evidence. First identify ongoing harm and propose the safest authorized containment while preserving logs/data. Establish affected versions, scope, timeline, expected/actual behavior and relevant recent changes. Separate facts, hypotheses, mitigation and confirmed correction. Design controlled experiments and avoid speculative repair of production data. Define verification from the user’s perspective and proportionate prevention. Do not take destructive, access-changing or deployment actions without explicit authority. Return current status, uncertainty and the next safe action.
```
