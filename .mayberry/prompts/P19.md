## P19 — Dependency review

**Use when:** A package/service addition or upgrade needs a decision.

**Inputs:** Proposed dependency/version, required capability and project constraints.

```text
Evaluate [dependency and version] for [actual need]. Check exact official identity, runtime/API compatibility, permissions, install/build behavior, license obligations, maintenance, known vulnerability information, and material effects inherited through its own dependencies (transitive effects). Compare existing platform/project alternatives. Separate verified facts from uncertainty and date-sensitive claims. Propose a bounded integration experiment, update owner and exit/replacement path. Do not install or modify lockfiles. Return accept/defer/reject with evidence and conditions, not a popularity-based recommendation.
```
