## P09 — Project roadmap

**Use when:** You need project-level sequencing rather than a task list.

**Inputs:** Product contract, architecture, research gates and constraints.

```text
Create a project roadmap from [accepted product contract, architecture and research results]. Define milestones by useful outcomes and exit evidence. Sequence blocking unknowns and risky assumptions early. Use vertical slices and show dependencies. Connect requirements to milestones and the next feature brief. Detail only the next executable slice; leave later implementation choices intentionally deferred. Include setup, integration, validation, release, recovery and maintenance obligations where relevant. Do not invent dates or turn future ideas into commitments. Do not implement.
```
