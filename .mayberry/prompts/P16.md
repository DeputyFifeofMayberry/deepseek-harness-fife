## P16 — Test review

**Use when:** A test suite may provide weak or misleading protection.

**Inputs:** Tests, intended behavior, implementation and known failures.

```text
Review [tests] against requirements and the affected implementation. For each material test, explain what realistic defect it catches and whether its expected result is independently justified. Find missing boundaries, over-mocking, brittle snapshots/selectors, nondeterminism, redundant cases and weakened assertions. Check whether the regression test fails on the original defect when that can be safely demonstrated. Do not inflate test count or claim execution you did not perform. Return evidence-backed findings and targeted changes; do not edit without authorization. Inspect discovery, selection filters, skips, first failures/retries, quarantine rules, suite runtime, historical fixtures and CI failure propagation. Review fixture/snapshot/recording, tolerance, benchmark baseline and gate changes separately from implementation. Require independent justification for changing an oracle; do not change expectations merely to obtain green results.
```
