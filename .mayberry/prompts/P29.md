## P29 — Final project review

**Use when:** A milestone, release or major transition needs a holistic final check.

**Inputs:** Full current project artifacts, requirements, roadmap, evidence and runbooks.

```text
Independently review [project/milestone] against its accepted purpose and supplied evidence. Check lifecycle completeness, requirement traceability, architecture, data/security, testing, actual artifacts, release/recovery, operations, maintenance, documentation and retirement obligations where relevant. Inspect the project rather than trusting prior summaries. Distinguish established defects, missing evidence, contextual tradeoffs and optional improvements. Do not redesign or expand scope. Return readiness with material corrections, owners/gates and limitations. Approval requires evidence; agreement between AI reviewers does not substitute for verification.
```
