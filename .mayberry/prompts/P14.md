## P14 — Root-cause analysis

**Use when:** A serious or repeated failure needs causal explanation.

**Inputs:** Timeline, reproduction, experiments and affected versions.

```text
Analyze [failure] using the supplied timeline, code and experiment evidence. Explain the causal mechanism and supporting intervention, contributing conditions, and why existing checks did not catch it. Do not force one root cause if the evidence supports interacting causes or unresolved uncertainty. Separate mitigation from correction. Propose a small set of preventive actions tied to the mechanism, with owners to be assigned and effectiveness checks. Do not invent observations, blame people without evidence or edit the system.
```
