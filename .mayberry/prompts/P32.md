## P32 — API and integration contract review

**Use when:** An internal boundary becomes durable or a third-party API/webhook is being integrated.

**Inputs:** Provider docs/version, actual consumers, schema/examples, constraints and evidence.

```text
Review [interface/integration] against inspected code and authoritative documentation for the applicable version. Define inputs/outputs and meaning, absent/null/default/unknown fields, safe error categories, authentication/authorization, timeouts, uncertain outcomes, idempotency, bounded retries, pagination and compatibility. For webhooks inspect signatures/replay rules, duplicate/out-of-order delivery, durable acknowledgment and recovery. Record external quotas, cost/data obligations and consumer versions. Do not invent endpoints or delivery guarantees. Propose contract and isolated real-boundary checks; mark mock-only or unrun evidence. Return W30 with confirmed rules, assumptions, gates and the simplest credible design. Do not call live write endpoints or implement.
```
