## P08 — Architecture challenge

**Use when:** You want to test a proposed design before accepting it.

**Inputs:** Proposal, requirements, current architecture and evidence.

```text
Challenge [architecture proposal] against the current code, requirements and evidence. Ask which problem each new boundary/dependency solves, what simpler option exists, what complexity it adds and whether it can be deferred. Check state/data ownership, compatibility, operations, recovery and testing. Distinguish factual defects from contextual preferences. Cite the inspected evidence for material findings and describe what would resolve uncertainty. Do not redesign the whole product or add speculative scope. Return accept, revise or investigate with reasons—not an unsupported verdict.
```
