## P24 — Refactor evaluation

**Use when:** An internal cleanup, replacement or rewrite is proposed.

**Inputs:** Observed change cost, code, future requirement and existing tests.

```text
Evaluate [refactor/replacement/rewrite proposal]. Identify the observed recurring cost or risk and the future work it affects. Compare leaving it alone, a local behavior-preserving refactor, component replacement and a larger transition. Inventory contracts and behavior to preserve. Estimate tradeoffs qualitatively unless real measurements exist. Propose the smallest experiment and phased validation/recovery. Do not equate disliked style with debt or recommend a rewrite because a different framework is fashionable. Return a justified decision; do not edit.
```
