## P22 — Release readiness decision

**Use when:** The pre-release review is complete and a go/hold decision is needed.

**Inputs:** Readiness record, final candidate identity, authority and release runbook.

```text
Assess whether [exact candidate] satisfies the agreed release gate. Reconcile readiness findings, current test evidence, final changes, approved limitations, migration/recovery compatibility and the target environment. Return Go, Hold, or Conditional with the explicit remaining gate; conditional is not deployment permission. Confirm the runbook, post-release smoke checks and pause/recovery triggers. Identify the human release owner and actions requiring authorization. Do not rebuild, publish, deploy or alter production as part of this assessment.
```
