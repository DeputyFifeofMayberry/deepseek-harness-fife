## P04 — Requirements creation

**Use when:** Intent needs observable acceptance criteria.

**Inputs:** Approved scope, workflows, constraints and evidence.

```text
Turn [approved product definition] into a compact product contract. Use stable requirement IDs, observable behavior and acceptance evidence. Cover relevant empty, invalid, denied, partial, interrupted and concurrent cases. Make quality targets meaningful by stating workload/environment and measurement, not invented numbers. Separate requirements from implementation preferences. Include non-goals and MVP boundaries. Flag ambiguous decisions rather than silently resolving consequential ones. Map each requirement to a proposed check. Do not edit code.
```
