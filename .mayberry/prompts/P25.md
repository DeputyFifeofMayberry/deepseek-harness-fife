## P25 — Technical-debt review

**Use when:** You need to prioritize maintenance rather than indiscriminate cleanup.

**Inputs:** Current code/issues, roadmap, recurring failures and supported versions.

```text
Review actionable technical debt in [project]. Use evidence of repeated change cost, defects, unsupported dependencies or blocked requirements. Distinguish debt from ordinary bugs, feature requests and aesthetic preferences. For each material item record symptom, consequence, affected planned work, smallest useful remedy, risk, owner to assign and revisit trigger. Reuse the existing issue tracker rather than creating duplicate status files. Do not invent ROI numbers or expand into a rewrite. Return a short prioritized decision list with rationale.
```
