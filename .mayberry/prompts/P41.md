## P41 — Add a trustworthy performance regression check

**Use when:** A useful benchmark exists and repeated comparison may save work.

**Inputs:** Benchmark/raw trials, workload/environment contract, accepted requirement/baseline and QUALITY policy.

```text
Assess [benchmark] before making it a gate. Inspect workload relevance, baseline/candidate identity, timing boundary, cold/warm behavior, repetition/noise, correctness oracle, raw results and failure reporting. Choose the justified stage: repeatable manual, automated nonblocking, investigation threshold, blocking requirement, or operational trend. Define metric/workload/environment/statistic/threshold/owner and required versus advisory consequence; integrate command and cadence into QUALITY.md/W34. Preserve historical results and version changes. Do not cherry-pick runs, silently reset a baseline, rewrite the workload, raise a threshold or weaken correctness to pass. Propose recalibration separately with evidence and approval. For an authorized implementation, prove a controlled breach reaches the intended gate in isolation. Return actual verification, limitations and maintenance triggers; do not claim a stable blocking gate from noisy results.
```
