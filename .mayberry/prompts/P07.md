## P07 — Architecture proposal

**Use when:** A demonstrated need may require a new boundary or mechanism.

**Inputs:** Requirement, current map, evidence, scale and constraints.

```text
Inspect the current system relevant to [requirement]. Propose the simplest adequate design at the project’s actual scale. Compare it with a credible local change or deferral. Explain responsibilities, interfaces, state/data ownership, dependencies, failure behavior, complexity introduced and reversal/transition cost. Identify any uncertain assumptions requiring a spike. Define verification and the condition that would justify future expansion. Do not introduce infrastructure merely for hypothetical scale. Return a proposed decision record; do not implement.
```
