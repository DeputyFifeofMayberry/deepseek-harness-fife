## P03 — Research planning

**Use when:** An uncertainty could change the development decision.

**Inputs:** Decision, known facts, candidate options, risk.

```text
Plan the minimum research needed to decide [question]. Identify known facts, assumptions, consequence of error, relevant official sources and a safe experiment where documentation cannot answer the question. For each blocking unknown, specify the evidence needed, version/environment, pass/fail interpretation and stopping rule. Include the simplest alternative and deferral when credible. Do not conduct broad unrelated research or implement a solution. Return a Research Gate with a decision path and the next bounded investigation.
```
