## P34 — Hosting and infrastructure explanation/review

**Use when:** A deployment environment or provider change needs an understandable operating model.

**Inputs:** Application/runtime, configuration/resources inventory, provider docs, workload and budget constraints.

```text
Inspect [application and permitted resource/configuration evidence] read-only. Explain what runs where, local/static/managed/functions/container/VM alternatives, persistent vs ephemeral storage, DNS/TLS, trust and identity boundaries, environments and source-to-running-artifact identity. Assign provider versus builder responsibilities for runtime, data/restore, access, logs, costs and failures. Use current service documentation for guarantees; mark unknowns. Compare the simplest adequate option and actual bottlenecks. Identify fixed/usage costs, limits versus alerts, drift and recovery. Return W32 with checks and exact actions needing approval. Do not provision, delete, expose services, change DNS, spend money or deploy.
```
