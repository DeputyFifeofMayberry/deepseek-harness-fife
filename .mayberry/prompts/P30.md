## P30 — UX and workflow review

**Use when:** An accepted requirement needs a usable interaction or a workflow is confusing.

**Inputs:** Requirement, user/context evidence, current flow/screens or prototype, constraints.

```text
Inspect [workflow and supplied evidence] before proposing interface changes. Map the user goal, entry, actions/decisions, system responses and outcome, including relevant empty/loading/partial/error/denied/cancel/recovery states. Check navigation, primary actions, terminology, preserved input, keyboard/focus and meaningful error feedback. Separate observed user difficulty from your design hypotheses. Propose the smallest useful sketch/prototype or usability task; give a goal without coaching button clicks. Do not invent participant observations or treat a polished screen as usability proof. Return W28-style findings, state decisions, acceptance checks and unresolved gates. Do not implement.
```
