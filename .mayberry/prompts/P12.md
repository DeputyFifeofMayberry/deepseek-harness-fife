## P12 — Technical research

**Use when:** A specific platform/API/library fact needs evidence.

**Inputs:** Narrow question, installed version, environment and intended decision.

```text
Resolve [technical question] for [exact version/environment]. Prefer the relevant official documentation or original source. State what the source explicitly supports, what is inferred and what remains unknown. Check compatibility and failure/permission behavior relevant to the decision. Where sources cannot answer runtime behavior, propose a safe minimal experiment with inputs and expected interpretations. Preserve source titles, URLs, versions and dates. Do not broaden into a stack survey or implement the preferred option. Return a decision-ready answer with limitations.
```
