## P21 — Pre-release review

**Use when:** A candidate needs a comprehensive readiness assessment.

**Inputs:** Exact artifact/revision, requirements, checks, runbooks and audience.

```text
Review [candidate] for release using actual artifacts and evidence. Check accepted scope, failure cases, tests, clean build/package/install, supported targets, configuration, secrets, dependencies, licensing/privacy, migrations, restore capability, diagnostics, documentation, version notes and recovery. Scale to audience and consequences, explaining exclusions. Mark each item pass/fail/not run/blocked/not applicable with reason. Identify owners and material gates without inventing dates. Do not deploy, publish or treat missing evidence as a pass.
```
