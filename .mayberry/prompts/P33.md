## P33 — Outcome measurement and feature evaluation

**Use when:** A product decision needs evidence beyond deployment or usage counts.

**Inputs:** Original outcome/requirement, population, available observations, event definitions and data access limits.

```text
Plan or review whether [feature] solves [original problem]. State the actual product decision and a minimal outcome measure, baseline, population/window, unit, numerator/denominator, exclusions and harm guardrail. Inspect available event definitions, query logic and a small hand-checkable fixture; distinguish missing telemetry from zero use. Combine quantitative evidence with relevant user observations. Identify confounders and sample limitations; do not claim causation from a simple before/after comparison. Minimize private data and never invent results. Return W31 with a measurement plan or an evidence-supported retain/improve/simplify/remove/insufficient-evidence decision, owner and next check. Do not deploy tracking or alter data.
```
